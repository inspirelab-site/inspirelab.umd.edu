Congratulations to [Linnea Hoheisel](/members/linnea-hoheisel) for receiving the Trainee Professional Development Award from the Society for Neuroscience! This competitive award recognizes undergraduate and graduate students and post baccalaureate and postdoctoral scholars who demonstrate scientific merit and excellence in research. As part of the award, Linnea will receive complimentary registration to Neuroscience 2026 and one year access to Neuronline. We look forward to seeing her present her work at the Early Career Poster Session this November!

In other news, congratulations to [Jianan Jian](/members/jianan-jian) for his oral presentation at EMBC! Jianan presented "Channel capacity as a dynamic effective connectivity measure for functional neuroimaging" in the session "Functional and Directed Connectivity Analysis I" while chairing for the session "Motor Neuroprostheses & FES". This presentation highlighted the innovative approach we proposed to quantify brain connectivity using a telecommunications metric, with its sensitivity and specificity validated using multimodal neural data. 

INSPIRE Lab is proud of the work Linnea and Jianan have been doing, and excited to see what's next!
